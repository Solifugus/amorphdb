## Business Model Summary

### Revenue Strategy
**Per-Node Subscription Pricing**
- Monthly subscription per node with fixed capacity
- Commitment discounts for 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year agreements
- Simple scaling model: more capacity/resilience = more nodes = more revenue

### Developer Ecosystem Strategy
**Equity Partnership Program**
- Partner with startups through equity stakes instead of cash payments
- Provide technical mentoring and business development support
- Create self-reinforcing ecosystem where developer success drives platform growth

### Market Positioning
**Unique Value Propositions**:
- First temporal database with multi-mesh bridge architecture
- Complete data provenance and audit trails
- Cross-organizational secure data collaboration
- Post-quantum security and zero-trust architecture

## Target Markets

## Key Differentiators

### Technical Advantages
- **Temporal-first architecture**: Never overwrites, maintains complete history
- **Multi-mesh bridges**: Secure cross-organizational data sharing
- **Reactive programming**: Automated computation through watchers
- **Business-friendly language**: MBL designed for domain experts

### Business Model Advantages
- **Aligned incentives**: Equity partnerships tie developer success to platform growth
- **Ecosystem lock-in**: Applications create switching costs and network effects
- **Simple pricing**: Per-node model easy to understand and budget
- **Scalable growth**: Node-based revenue scales with customer success
